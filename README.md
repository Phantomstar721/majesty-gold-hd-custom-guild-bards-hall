# Custom Guild: Bards Hall

Bring music, magic and daring swordplay to your Majesty Gold HD kingdom with a
three-level Bards Hall and three distinct, equally priced heroes.

## The bards

| Hero | Role | Abilities and progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Troubadour** | Companion support | Level 1: Ballad of Valor. Level 3: Marching Song. Level 5: Cutting Satire. Level 8: Heroic Refrain. |
| **Spellsinger** | Ranged magical combat | Level 1: Arcane Note. Level 3: Reverberation and shared Resonance. Level 5: Countermelody. Level 8: Resonant Burst and Crescendo. |
| **Blade Dancer** | Saber-and-dagger duelist | Level 1: Duelist Training. Level 3: Riposte. Level 5: Deflect. Level 8: Dueling Flourish, Rousing Finale and Encore. |

Troubadours prefer following active heroes. Their songs improve accuracy and
defense, quicken travel, weaken enemies, or strengthen allied damage. They earn
half of their companion's combat and exploration experience without taking any
away from that hero. A Troubadour cannot follow another Troubadour, and two
Troubadours cannot reserve the same companion for ordinary support.

Spellsingers explore, fight and perform. Arcane Notes and their echoes build
Resonance shared by all Spellsingers against that enemy, making further notes
more damaging. Resonant Burst consumes the buildup to stun enemies with a
Crescendo. Countermelody disrupts special spellcasting and reduces accuracy.

Blade Dancers hunt and duel with two weapon damage rolls in one attack. Parrying
can trigger Riposte; Deflect blocks missiles and direct magical attacks. At
level 8, every third successful hit against one opponent becomes a Flourish.
A Flourish against a monster prepares Finale; landing the killing blow inspires
nearby allies and banks Encore for the next monster or enemy-hero encounter.

Ordinary bard hunts favor the nearest opponent they consider manageable and
exclude civilians. Champion calls keep their priority.

Each bard has distinct names, behavior, world animations, portraits, spell and
enchantment icons, effects and eleven voiced reactions.

## Guild progression and earnings

All three bard types cost **500 gold** and are available from level 1.

| Completed guild level | Shared recruit places | New offerings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 | All three heroes and bard hiring |
| 2 | 6 | Ballad of Renown, Street Spectacle and Prize Duel |
| 3 | 8 | Epic Chronicles research |

Upgrade capacity becomes available when construction finishes.

- **Ballad of Renown:** Troubadours collect gold and experience for supporting
  successful bounties, lair destruction and qualifying monster victories.
- **Street Spectacle:** Spellsingers perform outdoors near town buildings,
  earning tips and experience from passing listeners.
- **Prize Duel:** Blade Dancers visit suitable friendly venues. They have a
  65% chance to win a cash prize; losing awards practice experience.

**Epic Chronicles** costs **3,000 gold once per kingdom** and grants your
kingdom's heroes **15% more earned gold and experience**. The bonus requires at
least one surviving, completed level-3 Bards Hall. Research knowledge survives
guild loss, so rebuilding to level 3 restores the bonus without another payment.
A Golden Chorus effect marks guilds sustaining the Chronicles.

## Hiring and equipment

Heroes can hire an existing bard for about **two game days**. The **200-gold**
fee splits **100 to the guild and 100 to the bard**; the patron must also retain
a 50-gold reserve. Hiring can be opened or closed in the recruitment panel.
Hired bards accompany their patron using the support appropriate to their type.

Troubadours and Spellsingers use **Instruments**; Blade Dancers use **Dual Wield**
weapons. Equipment follows ordinary upgrade shops and enchanting. Troubadours
and Blade Dancers wear stock leather armor. Spellsingers are unarmored like
Wizards and do not use poison or weapon coatings.

## Installation

Requires [Majesty Mod Manager](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793024054)
with support for the Bards Hall's declared features.

1. Make the package available in the Manager's local Mods or Workshop catalog.
2. Select **Custom Guild: Bards Hall**, along with your other compatible mods.
3. Choose **Prepare Selected Mods**.
4. Use **Launch Majesty** inside the Manager.

The recruitment interface, equipment and shared systems depend on the Manager
runtime. Launching the raw package through Majesty's ordinary mod selector does
not supply those features.

Designed for Original Majesty and Northern Expansion content. This release
candidate is undergoing final testing before publication.

## Artwork and voices

Includes custom three-stage building art, distinct sprites for all three bards,
matching interface artwork and custom spell effects. All three voice sets use
the selected actor recordings, including the revised Troubadour Level 10 and
Death lines.

The playable package is distributed through Workshop; building it from source
requires the private assets and a local Majesty Gold HD installation.
