# Custom Guild: Bards Hall

Every kingdom needs a story worth telling. Give yours a chorus.

Raise a Bards Hall and welcome three very different performers to Ardania:
the travelling Troubadour, the magical Spellsinger, and the daring Blade Dancer.
Each brings original artwork, animations, portraits, names, spell effects and
voiced reactions, along with a private set of habits and ambitions.

## A tale, a song, a duel

**Troubadours** follow busy heroes and turn their adventures into music. Ballad
of Valor inspires their companions, Marching Song quickens their steps, Cutting
Satire weakens their enemies, and Heroic Refrain strengthens their blows while
softening those they receive. Troubadours share their companions' combat and
exploration experience, and Ballad of Renown rewards tales of worthy victories.

**Spellsingers** make their voices into weapons. Arcane Note echoes between
enemies, leaving shared Resonance that makes further notes more powerful.
Countermelody disrupts hostile spellcasting; Resonant Burst consumes the
building harmony in a stunning Crescendo. Between adventures, a Street
Spectacle draws tips and experience from passing townsfolk and heroes.

**Blade Dancers** turn swordplay into performance. Saber and dagger strike
together; a skillful parry earns a Riposte, while Deflect turns aside missiles
and direct magic. Dueling Flourish sets up a Rousing Finale that inspires nearby
allies. A banked Encore carries that triumph into the next duel. Prize Duels
offer a purse for victory or experience for practice.

## Build the grandest hall

- Recruit any of the three bard types for **500 gold**.
- Completed guild levels provide **4, 6 and 8 shared places**.
- Level 2 unlocks Ballad of Renown, Street Spectacle and Prize Duel.
- Open hiring to let heroes engage an existing bard for about **two days**.
  The **200-gold** fee pays 100 to the guild and 100 to the hired bard.
- At completed level 3, research **Epic Chronicles** once for **3,000 gold**.
  Your kingdom's heroes then earn **15% more gold and experience** while a
  completed level-3 Bards Hall survives. Lose the halls, rebuild their grandeur,
  and the music returns without another research payment.

Instruments and Dual Wield equipment use ordinary upgrade shops and enchanting.
Troubadours and Blade Dancers wear leather; Spellsingers travel unarmored and
do not use weapon coatings.

## Required launcher

This mod requires [Majesty Mod Manager](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793024054).

Select **Custom Guild: Bards Hall** in the Manager, choose **Prepare Selected
Mods**, then use **Launch Majesty** there. Select your other compatible mods
alongside it to prepare one combined profile. The guild's recruitment panel,
equipment and shared systems require the Manager's runtime; enabling the raw
package in Majesty's ordinary mod selector does not provide those features.
